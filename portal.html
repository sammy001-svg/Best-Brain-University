Getting Your Account

Portal and email credentials are issued by the University once your registration is complete.

1

Complete Registration

Finish registration for the semester with the Registrar and the Business Office.

2

Collect Your Credentials

Request your official University email account and sign-in details from the Registrar’s office.

3

Sign In and Set a Password

Log in to webmail, change the temporary password immediately and keep it private.

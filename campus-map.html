Key Destinations on Campus

Once you arrive, these are the places most visitors and new students need to find.

Administration Block

The Office of the President, the Registrar and central administration. First stop for official business.

Admissions Office

Applications, entry requirements and campus tours for prospective students and their families.

Business Office

Tuition payments, fee clearance and receipts.

Lecture Halls

Teaching spaces shared across the University’s five colleges.

University Library

Study space, the research collection and access to digital resources.

Laboratories

Practical facilities supporting Information Technology and Health Sciences programs.

Student Centre

Student Affairs, campus organizations and the heart of student life.

Sports Grounds

Athletics and inter-college competition, including the annual Sports Day.

Getting Here

By Private Car

Take the Robertsfield Highway (RIA Road) towards Thinker’s Village and turn onto Guest House Road. Visitor parking is available on campus.

By Taxi or Commercial Transport

Vehicles running the RIA Road route stop at Thinker’s Village. From the junction, Guest House Road leads to the campus entrance.

From Roberts International Airport

The campus lies on the Monrovia side of the airport highway, making it a straightforward drive for visitors arriving from abroad.

Visiting the Campus

  • Report to the reception desk on arrival
  • Prospective students should arrange visits through the Office of Admissions
  • Administrative offices operate Monday to Friday during business hours
  • Call ahead to confirm that the office you need is open
  • Group and school visits can be arranged in advance

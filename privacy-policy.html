Best Brains International University respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website. This policy sets out, in plain terms, what information we collect, why we collect it and what we do with it.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy explains how Best Brains International University (“BBIU”, “the University”, “we”) handles personal information collected through this website. It covers the pages published at this domain.

It does not cover information you provide to the University in person, on paper, or through separate systems such as student records held by the Registrar, which are governed by the University’s own administrative procedures.

2. Information You Give Us

The website does not require you to create an account. You share personal information only when you choose to contact us — for example, through an enquiry form, an alumni registration, or by emailing or telephoning the University directly.

  • Your name and contact details
  • The category you identify with, such as prospective student, alumnus or partner
  • The subject of your enquiry and the content of your message
  • Any additional information you choose to include

3. Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, the servers hosting this site may record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This information is used to keep the site available and secure.

This site also loads fonts and icons from third-party providers (Google Fonts and a public content delivery network). When your browser requests those files, the providers may record your IP address under their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information you send us is used only to respond to you and to carry out the ordinary work of the University.

  • Answering enquiries about admissions, programs and University services
  • Processing applications and following up with prospective students
  • Maintaining the alumni register and inviting graduates to University events
  • Administering gifts and partnerships, and issuing receipts
  • Improving the content and accuracy of this website

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

Information is shared internally only with the office that needs it to answer your enquiry — for example, an admissions question is passed to the Office of Admissions. We may disclose information where we are required to do so by law, or where it is necessary to protect the safety of members of the University community.

6. Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as it is needed to deal with your request and to maintain a reasonable administrative record. Application and student records are retained under the University’s records procedures.

7. Your Choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct information that is wrong, or ask to be removed from mailing lists such as the alumni register. Write to us using the contact details at the end of this policy and we will respond within a reasonable period.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information in our care. No website or email system can be guaranteed completely secure, however, so please do not send sensitive information such as identification numbers or payment details through an ordinary enquiry message. Where such details are required, the relevant University office will tell you how to provide them safely.

9. Applicants Under 18

Some applicants and visitors to this site are under the age of eighteen. We ask that a parent or guardian be involved where a minor is submitting an application or an enquiry that involves personal details.

10. Links to Other Websites

This website links to external services, including the University webmail system and social media pages. Once you follow such a link, you are subject to the privacy practices of that provider, and this policy no longer applies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the website and the University’s services develop. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or about the information we hold, can be sent to info@bbiu.org.lr, telephoned to +231-770-370-894 or +231-881-324-323, or addressed to the University at Guest House Road, Thinker’s Village, RIA Road, Paynesville, Liberia.

Once a Best Brain, Always a Best Brain

A Community That Outlasts Graduation

Graduation changes your relationship with the University — it does not end it. Our alumni carry the values of the institution into classrooms, clinics, boardrooms, ministries and enterprises across Liberia and beyond.

The Alumni Network exists to keep that community connected: to open doors for one another, to mentor the students coming behind, and to strengthen the University that shaped them.

  • Stay connected with classmates and faculty
  • Mentor current students and recent graduates
  • Access campus facilities and University events
  • Support scholarships and campus development

What the Network Offers

Membership is open to every graduate of Best Brains International University, across all colleges and every graduating class.

Lifelong Network

Connect with fellow graduates across the colleges of Business, Information Technology, Education, Health Sciences and Liberal Arts.

Career Support

Job leads, professional development and continued access to the University’s career services long after graduation.

Mentorship

Guide current students through internships, attachments and their first steps into the world of work.

Library Access

Continued access to the University Library, its digital resources and the BBIU journals collection.

Reunions & Events

Homecoming gatherings, convocations and the University community events held each semester.

Ways to Give Back

Fund a scholarship, sponsor a laboratory, or support the students who now sit where you once sat.

Join the Alumni Register

Tell us where life has taken you. Registering keeps you on the list for reunions, mentorship calls and University news.

Your details are prepared as an email to info@bbiu.org.lr for the Alumni Relations desk.

Get Involved

  • Speak at a career day or class session
  • Host an intern or offer an attachment placement
  • Recruit graduates into your organization
  • Join a class committee for the next reunion
  • Contribute to the student scholarship fund

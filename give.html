Why Your Support Matters

Invest in the Next Generation of Liberian Leaders

Best Brains International University exists to build the mental capacity and moral character of young people so that they can take a meaningful part in the nation building process. That work depends on more than tuition alone.

Every gift — from an individual, an alumnus, a church, a company or a development partner — goes directly into the things students feel: a place in a classroom, a working laboratory, a book on the shelf, a lecturer with the training to teach well.

Speak to the Business Office

Where Your Gift Goes

You may direct your support to a specific area, or give to the general fund and let the University apply it where the need is greatest.

Student Scholarships

Tuition support for capable students whose circumstances would otherwise keep them out of the classroom.

Library & Learning Resources

Books, journals, digital subscriptions and study spaces for the University Library.

Laboratories & Technology

Computers, equipment and connectivity for the Information Technology and Health Sciences programs.

Campus Development

Classrooms, facilities and infrastructure that expand what the University can offer.

Faculty Development

Training, further study and professional development for the people who teach.

Research & Journals

Support for scholarship, publication and the BBIU journals programme.

Ways to Give

One-Time Gift

A single contribution of any size, directed to the area of your choosing.

Recurring Support

A regular monthly or termly commitment, which allows the University to plan ahead with confidence.

Sponsor a Student

Underwrite the tuition of a named student for a semester, an academic year or a full program.

Corporate Partnership

Institutional partnerships covering scholarships, equipment, internships and staff development.

Gifts in Kind

Books, computers, laboratory equipment, furniture and other resources the University can put to immediate use.

How to Arrange Your Gift

All giving is arranged through the University Business Office, which will confirm the available payment channels and issue a receipt for your contribution.

  • Contact the Business Office to discuss your gift
  • Tell us which area you would like to support
  • Receive confirmed payment details and a receipt
  • Get an account of how your gift was used

What Is Happening on Campus

The highlights below are drawn from the official 2025/2026 academic calendar. For the complete schedule of every registration date, examination week and holiday, see the academic calendar.

Aug 10 – 21
Academic

Registration Period for Returning Students

Returning students complete registration for the first semester with their academic advisors and the Business Office.

Aug 24 – 28
Academic

New Student Orientation Week

A week of introductions to the University: academic expectations, campus services, student organizations and campus life.

Sep 1
Academic

First Day of Classes

Instruction begins for Semester I across all colleges.

Sep 14
Deadline

Last Day to Add or Drop Classes

Final date for students to adjust their course load for the semester without penalty.

Oct 19 – 23
Academic

Mid-Term Examination Week

Mid-semester assessments across all programs.

Dec 14 – 18
Academic

Final Examination Week

End-of-semester examinations, concluding Semester I.

Mar 6
Community

Faculty Development Workshop

Professional development for teaching staff ahead of the second semester.

Mar 13
Academic

Student Orientation — Old and New

Orientation for continuing and newly admitted students entering Semester II.

Mar 16
Academic

Commencement of Classes

Semester II instruction begins.

Apr 1
Community

“The Gathering” for the University Community

A recurring community assembly bringing students, faculty and staff together during the semester.

May 1
Community

Sports Day

A full day of athletics and inter-college competition on the campus grounds.

Jun 10
Community

“Closing Gathering” for the University Community

The final community assembly of the academic year.

Jun 28
Ceremony

Baccalaureate Service

A service of thanksgiving for the graduating class and their families.

Ceremony

Senior Banquet

A celebration honouring the graduating seniors of the academic year.

The University’s graduation ceremony, at which degrees are conferred.

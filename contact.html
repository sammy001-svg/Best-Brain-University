Find the Campus

The University sits along the Robertsfield Highway corridor in Paynesville, just off Guest House Road in Thinker’s Village — a short drive from central Monrovia.

  • Guest House Road, Thinker’s Village, RIA Road
  • Paynesville City, Montserrado County, Liberia
  • Accessible by private car, taxi and commercial transport
  • Visitors should report to the reception desk on arrival

Who to Contact

Every enquiry reaches the University through the central email and telephone lines below. Address your message to the relevant office and it will be routed accordingly.

Office Handles Address Your Message To
Office of Admissions Applications, entry requirements, transfer of credits, campus visits The Director of Admissions
Office of the Registrar Registration, transcripts, records, graduation clearance The University Registrar
Business Office Tuition, fees, payment plans, receipts The Business Manager
Office of Student Affairs Student welfare, organizations, campus activities The Dean of Students
University Library Borrowing, research support, digital resources The University Librarian
Alumni Relations Alumni register, reunions, mentorship, giving The Alumni Relations Desk

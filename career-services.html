Home Student Life Career Services

From Classroom to Career

A degree opens the door; preparation carries you through it. Career Services works with students from their first year to the day they start work — and beyond.

Career Counselling

One-to-one guidance on choosing a major, mapping a career path and making decisions about further study.

CV & Application Support

Practical help building a professional CV, writing application letters and preparing supporting documents.

Interview Preparation

Mock interviews, practice questions and coaching on professional presentation and workplace communication.

Internships & Attachments

Placement guidance connecting students with organizations where they can apply what they are learning.

Entrepreneurship Support

For students building their own ventures — guidance on planning, structure and taking a first idea to market.

Graduate Study Advising

Advice on postgraduate options, scholarship applications and academic references.

How to Get Started

1

Book a Consultation

Contact the University and ask for the Career Services desk to arrange your first session.

2

Build Your Profile

Work through your CV, portfolio and application materials with a career advisor.

3

Gain Experience

Take on an internship, attachment or campus leadership role that builds your record.

4

Enter the Market

Apply with confidence, supported by interview practice and our employer network.

For Students & Graduates

Career Services is available to every enrolled student and to graduates of the University.

  • Career planning from your first year of study
  • CV, cover letter and portfolio reviews
  • Interview practice and professional etiquette
  • Guidance on internships and industrial attachments
  • Referrals and references for job applications
  • Continued support after graduation through the Alumni Network
Book a Consultation

For Employers & Partners

Organizations across Liberia recruit from Best Brains International University’s colleges of Business, Information Technology, Education, Health Sciences and Liberal Arts. We are glad to work with you.

  • Advertise vacancies and internships to our students
  • Host interns and industrial attachment placements
  • Speak at a career day or professional seminar
  • Partner on training and skills development
  • Sponsor a scholarship or an award of excellence
